# JournalPreview

**Purpose.** Blog or journal entry preview. Figma: `journal preview` (5:161).

**When to use.** Under a post photo in journal lists.

| Prop | Type | Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `date` · `title` · `meta` · `excerpt` | `string` | — | |
| `href` | `string` | — | Makes the title a link |

**Variants.** None.

**Tokens.** `border/strong`, `text/primary`, `text/secondary`, `text/accent`, `action/link` (hover).

**Do.** Put reading time or trip length in `date`.
**Don't.** Exceed two lines of excerpt.
